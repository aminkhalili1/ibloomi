About iBloomi

At iBloomi, we believe every bouquet should be as unique as the person receiving it. By combining artificial intelligence with floral creativity, we enable customers to design, preview, and purchase custom bouquets in a simple and intuitive way.

Our AI-powered platform helps users select flowers, colors, wrapping, and vases while creating beautiful, balanced arrangements with confidence. Our mission is to make custom floral design accessible to everyone, and our vision is a future where every bouquet is personal, transparent, and uniquely meaningful.
