About iBloomi

iBloomi is a floral technology company that integrates artificial intelligence with the floral industry. We believe every bouquet should be as unique as the occasion it celebrates, and that creating a custom bouquet should be clear, simple and intuitive.

Our platform offers AI-powered bouquet customization, allowing flowers, colors, wrapping and vases to be selected and previewed visually before an order is placed. Connected to real inventory and live pricing, every design is both available and accurately priced. Our QR message feature adds a personal, digital dimension to each bouquet, allowing a heartfelt message to accompany the gift.

Our mission is to make custom floral design accessible to everyone. Our vision is a future where every bouquet is personal, transparent and uniquely meaningful.
