Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit ibloomi.nl. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and improving our platform.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies are required for the platform to function correctly, such as keeping you logged in and remembering your bouquet design session.

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with iBloomi, so we can improve the experience. We use anonymized data only.

Preference cookies remember your settings and choices within the bouquet builder.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect how iBloomi functions.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party services that set their own cookies (e.g., analytics providers). These are subject to the respective providers' privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@ibloomi.nl.
