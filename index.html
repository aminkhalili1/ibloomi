Questions

Frequently asked

What is iBloomi?

iBloomi is an AI-powered bouquet builder that helps florists offer personalized bouquet design, real-time previews, and smarter custom ordering.

Who is iBloomi built for?

iBloomi is built for florists, flower shops, and floral businesses that want to modernize custom bouquet sales.

How does iBloomi help florists?

It reduces consultation time, helps customers visualize their bouquets, minimizes order mistakes, and supports more efficient custom bouquet sales.

Can customers design bouquets themselves?

Yes. Customers can select flowers, colors, wrapping, vase options, and preferences through a guided AI-powered flow.

Does iBloomi generate bouquet previews?

Yes. iBloomi creates AI-powered bouquet previews so customers can see their design before placing an order.

Can iBloomi work with my flower inventory?

Yes. iBloomi can be connected to your available flowers, colors, and stock levels so customers see relevant bouquet options.

Does iBloomi support automatic pricing?

Yes. Bouquet prices can be calculated based on selected flowers, size, packaging, and add-ons.

Is iBloomi suitable for in-store and online sales?

Yes. Florists can use iBloomi both as an online ordering tool and as an in-store consultation assistant.
