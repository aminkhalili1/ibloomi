Questions

Frequently asked

What is iBloomi?

iBloomi is an AI-powered bouquet builder that allows customers to create personalized flower arrangements through an interactive design experience.

How does iBloomi work?

iBloomi guides you through the bouquet creation process using AI assistance, helping you design a bouquet that matches your preferences and occasion while providing real-time visualization and transparent pricing.

Do I need floral design experience?

Not at all. iBloomi is designed for everyone. The platform provides intelligent guidance to help you create beautiful bouquets with confidence.

Can I customize my bouquet?

Yes. iBloomi is built around personalization, allowing you to create a bouquet that reflects your style, preferences, and gifting needs.

Will I see my bouquet before placing an order?

Yes. iBloomi provides a visual preview during the design process, helping you make informed decisions before purchasing.
