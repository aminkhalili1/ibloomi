Questions

Frequently asked

How does the AI bouquet builder work?

You tell iBloomi about the occasion, your preferred style, and your budget. The AI then suggests flowers, colors, and arrangements that fit your criteria. You can accept its suggestions, swap items, or customize every detail yourself. A live preview updates as you make changes.

Do I need to know anything about flowers?

Not at all. iBloomi's AI handles all the floral expertise — from seasonal availability to color harmony. You just make choices based on what you love. The AI explains each suggestion in plain language so you always know why something works.

What occasions can I design a bouquet for?

Any occasion: birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, sympathy gestures, graduations, new arrivals, or simply a gift for someone special. iBloomi adapts its recommendations based on the occasion you select.

Is there a minimum or maximum budget?

iBloomi works across a wide range of budgets. Whether you're looking to spend €20 or €200+, the AI finds arrangements that look beautiful at your price point. You can also set a maximum budget and iBloomi will stay within it.

When will iBloomi launch?

We're currently in our final development phase and preparing for launch. Join our waitlist to be among the first to access the platform and get an exclusive early access discount.

Will iBloomi be available in the Netherlands?

Yes! The Netherlands is our home market and the first region where iBloomi will be available. We'll expand to other markets based on demand.
