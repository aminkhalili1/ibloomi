Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Introduction

iBloomi ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform at ibloomi.nl.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name and email address when you join our waitlist or create an account. We may also collect usage data and preferences when you interact with the bouquet builder.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, send you updates about iBloomi, and personalize your bouquet design experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Google Analytics and Your Consent

iBloomi uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our public website and improve its content and experience. Google Analytics is optional: its script is not loaded, and optional analytics tracking is not activated, before you give your consent through the cookie preferences banner. You can consent by selecting Accept, or by enabling Analytics under Adjust settings and saving your preferences.

If you select Refuse, Google Analytics is not loaded by our consent module. We remember your preference in browser storage so it applies on future visits. If you previously accepted, Google Analytics may load on your next visit based on your saved choice. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the Cookie Policy, using the Change cookie preferences link. When consent is withdrawn, the site reloads to stop already-loaded Google Analytics code and attempts to remove related first-party analytics cookies. Essential website functions remain available without optional analytics.

If analytics is enabled, Google may process data about visits and website interactions in accordance with its applicable terms and policies. For more information about cookies, our consent choices and third-party services, please read our Cookie Policy.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in the European Union. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as applicable to users in the Netherlands and the EU.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ibloomi.nl.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach us at privacy@ibloomi.nl or through our contact form at ibloomi.nl.
