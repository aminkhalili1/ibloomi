Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

iBloomi ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform at ibloomi.nl.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name and email address when you join our waitlist or create an account. We may also collect usage data and preferences when you interact with the bouquet builder.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, send you updates about iBloomi, and personalize your bouquet design experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in the European Union. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as applicable to users in the Netherlands and the EU.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ibloomi.nl.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach us at privacy@ibloomi.nl or through our contact form at ibloomi.nl.
