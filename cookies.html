Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files that websites can store on your device. Websites can also use browser storage to remember settings. iBloomi uses certain technologies to support essential functionality, and requests your permission before activating optional analytics.

Essential and functional technologies

These support functions such as sign-in and account security, remembering essential settings and saving your cookie consent choice. They are not controlled by the optional analytics switch. The consent manager stores your preference in your browser's local storage under ibloomi.analytics-consent.v1.

Other necessary storage may be used by services involved in authentication and core site functionality. Disabling essential browser storage may prevent some features from working.

Optional analytics — Google Analytics

With your permission, iBloomi uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-70S9FK30TR) to understand visits and interactions with the website and help improve the experience. Depending on Google's configuration and browser settings, this may involve analytics cookies such as _ga and related cookies. Analytics is off by default and the Google Analytics script is not loaded by this website until you accept it.

If you reject optional analytics, our consent module does not load Google Analytics. If you withdraw consent after accepting, the website reloads to stop already-running analytics code and attempts to remove first-party Google Analytics cookies.

Marketing and advertising

The iBloomi consent module does not install advertising or marketing trackers and does not request marketing consent. If new optional technologies are added, this policy and consent choices should be reviewed before those technologies are activated.

Your choices

When you first visit, choose Accept to allow optional analytics, Reject to refuse it, or Customize cookies to review the categories and save a preference. Essential website functions remain available after rejecting analytics.

You can change or withdraw your decision at any time using the Cookie settings button shown on the site after a choice has been saved. You can also clear cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. For further information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Other third-party services may support essential functions such as account authentication. Their data processing is described in the relevant privacy notices. This policy describes the consent controls used on the public iBloomi website; any separate Bouquet Builder service should be assessed independently.
